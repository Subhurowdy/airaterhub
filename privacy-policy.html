Privacy Policy

Template for site-owner review — update the date and verify all details before publishing.

1. Information processed

When a visitor submits a URL for analysis, the current AiraterHub backend may process that URL, retrieve publicly accessible HTML from the target page, and calculate page/content metrics. The application is configured to store the submitted URL, overall score, report data and timestamp in a local SQLite database.

The current database schema also supports user accounts, but account registration, login, payment processing and account settings must not be described as active unless they have actually been implemented and tested.

2. How information is used

Submitted information is used to generate and store analysis reports, operate the service, diagnose errors and improve reliability. Do not submit URLs that contain private tokens, passwords or confidential information.

3. Public pages and third-party websites

AiraterHub may retrieve a public web page that a user submits. The destination website may separately collect information under its own privacy policy. AiraterHub does not control third-party websites.

4. Storage and retention

Analysis records are stored in the application's SQLite database until they are deleted by an authorized process or removed under the site's retention practices. The site owner should define and publish a retention period and backup/deletion procedure before launch.

5. Cookies and sessions

Do not claim that login/session cookies are used until authentication has been implemented. If sessions, analytics, advertising or other cookies are added, update this policy to describe them and provide any legally required choices or notices.

6. Sharing and security

The site owner should document any hosting providers, analytics providers, email services or payment processors that receive personal data. Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no online service can promise absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

The service is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe personal information was submitted improperly, contact the site owner.

8. Your choices and contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the site owner using the real support email published on the Contact page. Applicable rights depend on the user's location and relevant law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the product's features or data practices change. Publish the date of the latest revision after reviewing this document.
