Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Process

When you use AiraterHub to analyze a URL, our backend retrieves publicly accessible HTML from the submitted page and calculates content, SEO, and readability metrics. We store the analyzed URL, overall score, generated report, and timestamp to provide analysis history and improve service performance.

2. How Information Is Used

We use the processed information solely to generate real-time analysis reports, maintain platform reliability, prevent misuse, and enhance our algorithms. We advise users not to submit URLs containing confidential tokens, session IDs, or private data.

3. Third-Party Websites

AiraterHub analyzes public web pages at your request. Destination websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies. AiraterHub does not control or assume responsibility for external content or practices.

4. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

AiraterHub may use cookies to improve user experience and deliver relevant advertisements.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to AiraterHub or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect stored analysis data. However, no data transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

AiraterHub does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe such information has been collected, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or data requests, please reach out to us at support@airaterhub.com.
