Frequently Asked Questions

What does AiraterHub analyze?

It evaluates signals available from a submitted public HTML page, such as metadata, headings, text structure and readability indicators. The exact signals depend on what the page exposes.

Are scores guaranteed?

No. Scores are heuristic estimates, not guarantees of ranking, reach, engagement or virality.

Why might a URL fail?

A page may block automated requests, require sign-in, return non-HTML content, redirect, or limit access. Try a publicly accessible page you are authorized to analyze.

Does it support login and Pro?

Profile, authentication, settings and verified subscription/payment features are still being completed. Do not rely on them as active until they have been tested end-to-end.

How do I contact support?

You can contact our support team at support@airaterhub.com or visit our Contact page.

Can I analyze private content?

Only submit content you are authorized to access. Do not submit private URLs containing access tokens or confidential information.
